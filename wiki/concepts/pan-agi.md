# PAN AGI (Physical, Agentic, Nested)

## Definition

A proposed architecture for general-purpose artificial general intelligence based on a specific world model design. PAN stands for **Physical** (grounded in multimodal sensory experience), **Agentic** (optimized for goal-oriented decision-making and planning), and **Nested** (hierarchical, multi-level representations supporting reasoning at multiple levels of abstraction). PAN AGI integrates world modeling with reinforcement learning to enable a single cognitive architecture performing diverse tasks (household chores, risky expeditions, research investigations, social navigation, complex enterprises).

## Why It Matters

PAN AGI proposes a concrete architectural instantiation addressing limitations in existing world modeling approaches. It emphasizes that a general-purpose AI system must be grounded in physical interaction, designed for agentic planning (not passive observation), and capable of hierarchical reasoning across levels of abstraction. The "nested" aspect reflects that humans operate with hierarchies of abilities from immediate (body control) to abstract (planning, strategy).

## Variants / Approaches

- **Hierarchical generative modeling**: Layer representations from low-level (pixels, actions) to high-level (goals, strategies)
- **Enhanced LLM backbone**: Use language models as the core reasoning substrate, augmented with world model capabilities
- **Generative latent predictive architecture**: Predict at multiple levels of abstraction simultaneously
- **Multimodal training data**: Incorporate vision, proprioception, language, and interaction data
- **Reinforcement learning integration**: Use simulated world model trajectories for agent training and refinement

## Evidence

- The Critiques of World Models paper proposes PAN AGI as an alternative architecture addressing identified shortcomings in prior approaches [[sources/critiques-of-world-models]]
- PAN AGI is grounded in five design principles: multimodal data, mixed continuous/discrete representations, hierarchical generation, observation-grounded loss, RL-based agent training [[sources/critiques-of-world-models]]
- The architecture addresses the goal of simulating actionable possibilities across diverse scenarios [[sources/critiques-of-world-models]]
