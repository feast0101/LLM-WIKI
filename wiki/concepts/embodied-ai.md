# Embodied AI

## Definition

Embodied AI refers to artificial intelligence systems that are grounded in physical or virtual environments and learn through interaction with those environments. Embodied agents couple perception and action—they see the world, reason about it, and take actions that produce observable consequences. This contrasts with disembodied AI (like language models) that process abstract symbols without direct environmental interaction.

## Why It Matters

Embodied AI is the missing link in AI's evolution from pure symbolic reasoning to genuine understanding. Language models are disembodied—they excel at pattern matching and reasoning about concepts but lack sensorimotor grounding. Embodied AI, grounded in spatial intelligence and world models, enables machines to understand cause-and-effect, physics, and the consequences of actions. This is essential for robots, autonomous vehicles, and eventually artificial general intelligence.

## Variants / Approaches

- **Simulation-based embodied learning** — Agents learn in virtual environments before real-world deployment
- **Robot learning from interaction** — Direct learning from physical robot experience
- **Multimodal embodied cognition** — Integrating vision, language, and motor control
- **Self-supervised embodied learning** — Learning representations through environmental interaction
