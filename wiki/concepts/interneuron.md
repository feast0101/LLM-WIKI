# Interneuron

## Definition

An interneuron is a type of neuron that communicates with other neurons, rather than directly receiving sensory input or commanding muscles. Interneurons are the primary constituent of the central nervous system, making up the vast majority of brain neurons where information processing occurs.

## Why It Matters

Interneurons comprise the computational substrate of the brain. They interconnect with each other and with sensory and motor neurons, forming networks that perform all cognitive and motor computations. Most neurons in the brain are interneurons.

## Variants / Approaches

- **Excitatory interneurons**: Drive downstream neurons to fire.
- **Inhibitory interneurons**: Suppress downstream neural activity.
- **Modulatory interneurons**: Alter the gain or threshold of downstream neurons.

## Evidence

- Most types of neurons in the nervous system are interneurons—neurons that communicate with other neurons. [[sources/neuralink-and-the-brains-magical-future]]
- When you think, it is interneurons in the brain talking to each other. [[sources/neuralink-and-the-brains-magical-future]]
