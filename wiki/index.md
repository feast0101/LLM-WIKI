## Concepts (53)

**Agentic World Modeling - Levels × Laws Taxonomy:**
- [[concepts/predictor]] — L1 Capability Level: learns one-step, local transition operators without composing into multi-step rollouts
- [[concepts/simulator]] — L2 Capability Level: composes transitions into multi-step rollouts respecting domain laws; enables agent planning
- [[concepts/evolver]] — L3 Capability Level: autonomously revises model when predictions fail; learns from distributional shift
- [[concepts/levels-laws-taxonomy]] — Two-axis framework (L1/L2/L3 × physical/digital/social/scientific) organizing agentic world models
- [[concepts/physical-law]] — Governing-law regime: world models respecting Newton's laws and physical dynamics
- [[concepts/digital-law]] — Governing-law regime: world models respecting program semantics and computational logic
- [[concepts/social-law]] — Governing-law regime: world models respecting agent beliefs, goals, and social norms
- [[concepts/scientific-law]] — Governing-law regime: world models respecting causal mechanisms and experimental constraints

**Critiques & Alternative Architectures:**
- [[concepts/hypothetical-thinking]] — Ability to mentally simulate multiple possible outcomes of actions for goal-oriented reasoning
- [[concepts/actionable-possibilities]] — Set of plausible futures reachable through different action sequences; primary goal of world models
- [[concepts/pan-agi]] — PAN (Physical, Agentic, Nested) architecture for general-purpose AGI based on hierarchical world modeling
- [[concepts/hierarchical-representations]] — Multi-level representational structures enabling reasoning from sensorimotor to abstract levels
- [[concepts/generative-learning]] — Training framework where models learn to generate simulated futures; self-supervised, observation-grounded
- [[concepts/simulative-reasoning]] — Agent decision-making using world models to imagine futures and select best action sequences

**World Models & Spatial Intelligence:**
- [[concepts/world-models]] — Learned representations of space and time enabling AI to understand, simulate, and reason about environments
- [[concepts/renderer]] — World model that outputs pixels for human consumption; optimizes for visual fidelity
- [[concepts/planner]] — World model that outputs actions; decides what agents should do given observations and goals
- [[concepts/spatial-intelligence]] — Capacity to understand, reason about, and interact with physical and virtual worlds in 3D
- [[concepts/physics-engine]] — Computational system that simulates physical interactions, enforcing Newton's laws and dynamics
- [[concepts/gaussian-splats]] — Neural rendering technique representing 3D scenes as Gaussian basis functions for efficient view synthesis
- [[concepts/pomdp]] — Partially Observable Markov Decision Process; foundational framework for agent-environment interaction
- [[concepts/marble]] — World Labs' foundation model generating 3D environments from multimodal prompts with visual and physics outputs
- [[concepts/embodied-ai]] — AI systems grounded in physical or virtual environments, coupling perception and action
- [[concepts/3d-understanding]] — Ability to perceive, represent, and reason about three-dimensional structure and spatial relationships

**Neuroscience & Brain Structure:**
- [[concepts/brain-machine-interface]] — Direct communication pathway between brain and external devices via neural signal recording and decoding
- [[concepts/neuron]] — Specialized cell transmitting electrical and chemical signals; fundamental unit of neural computation
- [[concepts/action-potential]] — Rapid electrical pulse propagating along axon; carries information through nervous system at 1-100 m/s
- [[concepts/synapse]] — Junction between neurons where information transfer occurs via neurotransmitters
- [[concepts/neurotransmitter]] — Chemical messenger released at synapses; excites or inhibits downstream neurons
- [[concepts/myelin-sheath]] — Fatty insulation wrapping axons; increases action potential speed from 1 m/s to 100 m/s
- [[concepts/neural-network]] — Interconnected system of neurons forming basis of brain function and computation
- [[concepts/neuroplasticity]] — Ability of brain to restructure itself through learning and experience; basis of skill acquisition
- [[concepts/cortex]] — Outer layer of brain (~nickel thickness) containing 15-20 billion neurons; locus of cognition and motor control
- [[concepts/gray-matter]] — Brain tissue composed of neuron cell bodies; performs computation
- [[concepts/white-matter]] — Brain tissue composed of myelinated axons; carries signals between brain regions
- [[concepts/central-nervous-system]] — Brain and spinal cord; primary computational center of nervous system
- [[concepts/peripheral-nervous-system]] — Sensory and motor neurons connecting CNS to body; afferent and efferent pathways
- [[concepts/sensory-neuron]] — Neuron transmitting information from sensory receptors to CNS; can extend up to 1 meter
- [[concepts/motor-neuron]] — Neuron transmitting commands from CNS to muscles; each brain has unique motor code
- [[concepts/interneuron]] — Neuron communicating with other neurons; comprises most of brain's neurons

**Learning Modes & Cognitive Processes:**
- [[concepts/focused-mode]] — State of deep concentration on a specific problem; uses prefrontal cortex for analytical thinking
- [[concepts/diffuse-mode]] — Relaxed mental state with wandering attention; enables creative insights and novel connections
- [[concepts/chunking]] — Compressing multiple ideas into single meaningful unit; core mechanism of expertise and learning
- [[concepts/active-recall]] — Retrieving knowledge from memory without cues; far more effective than passive review

**Learning Techniques & Strategies:**
- [[concepts/spaced-repetition]] — Reviewing material at increasing intervals; leverages spacing effect for durable memory
- [[concepts/pomodoro-technique]] — 25-minute focused work blocks with short breaks; addresses procrastination via process focus
- [[concepts/memory-palace]] — Spatial mnemonic technique placing information at locations in imagined space
- [[concepts/mnemonics]] — Memory devices encoding information for easier retrieval (acronyms, rhymes, vivid imagery)
- [[concepts/input-hygiene]] — Skimming material before deep study to prime brain with conceptual framework
- [[concepts/illusion-of-competence]] — False sense of learning from passive study (rereading, highlighting)

**Cognitive & Lifestyle Factors:**
- [[concepts/procrastination]] — Voluntary task delay driven by emotional discomfort; addressed via process-focused techniques
- [[concepts/sleep-and-learning]] — Sleep consolidates memories and activates diffuse mode; critical for learning effectiveness
- [[concepts/exercise-and-learning]] — Physical activity enhances cognition via increased blood flow, neurotrophic factors, and stress reduction
