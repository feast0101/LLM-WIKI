# Fei-Fei Li

## What

Fei-Fei Li is a pioneering AI researcher and entrepreneur who has emerged as a leading voice in spatial intelligence and world models. She is the founder of World Labs, a company focused on developing foundational world model technology. Li's career has spanned academic research at Stanford and leadership roles at tech companies, with a consistent focus on building AI systems that understand the physical world.

## Context

Li is central to the spatial intelligence research agenda in this wiki. She authored two major essays ingested in the current round: a foundational taxonomy of world models (outlining renderers, simulators, and planners) and a manifesto arguing that spatial intelligence is AI's next frontier. Her work bridges academic computer vision research with commercial product development, making her a key figure at the intersection of theory and practice in 3D AI.
