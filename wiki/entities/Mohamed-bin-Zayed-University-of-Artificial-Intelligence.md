# Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence

## What

Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI) is a research university in the UAE dedicated to AI research and education. It is home to the Institute of Foundation Models where Eric Xing and co-authors conduct research on world modeling and AGI systems.

## Context

MBZUAI is a leading institution for AI research with a focus on foundation models and general-purpose AI systems, evidenced by their contributions to world modeling theory and the PAN AGI architecture.
