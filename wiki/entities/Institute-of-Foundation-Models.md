# Institute of Foundation Models

## What

The Institute of Foundation Models is a research institute at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence, home to Eric Xing and co-authors working on world modeling research and the PAN AGI architecture.

## Context

The Institute of Foundation Models focuses on fundamental research in foundation models and AI systems, including world modeling approaches that enable general-purpose AI.
