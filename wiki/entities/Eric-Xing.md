# Eric Xing

## What

Eric Xing is a machine learning researcher and co-author of the "Critiques of World Models" paper. He is affiliated with the Institute of Foundation Models at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence and Carnegie Mellon University.

## Context

Eric Xing is a prominent voice in world modeling research, contributing critical analysis of existing approaches and proposing the PAN AGI (Physical, Agentic, Nested) architecture for general-purpose artificial intelligence grounded in world modeling.
