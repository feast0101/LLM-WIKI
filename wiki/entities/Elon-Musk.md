# Elon Musk

## What

Elon Musk is an entrepreneur and engineer who founded Neuralink, a company focused on developing brain-machine interfaces. In the context of neurotechnology, he is positioned as attempting to build a "wizard hat for the brain"—a device that would create a direct interface between human consciousness and computers.

## Context

In the Neuralink article, Musk serves as the visionary behind the company's mission to enable humans to integrate directly with artificial intelligence. The article frames him alongside his other ventures (Tesla, SpaceX) as someone whose companies aim to redefine both what humans will *do* and what humans will *be*. He is characterized as combining bold technical ambition with a clear vision of humanity's technological future.
