# Tim Urban

## What

Tim Urban is the writer and founder of Wait But Why, a popular blog known for deep-dive explanations of complex scientific and technological concepts. He wrote the comprehensive analysis "Neuralink and the Brain's Magical Future" published in April 2017.

## Context

Urban's article is a detailed exploration of neurotechnology, brain science, and Neuralink's mission. He is known for making complex topics accessible through narrative structure, visual analogies, and multifaceted framing that zooms both in on technical details and out to philosophical implications.
