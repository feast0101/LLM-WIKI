# National University of Singapore

## What

The National University of Singapore is a major research university and institutional home of multiple core contributors to the "Agentic World Modeling" survey. Key contributors include Xuan Billy Zhang (project lead), Lingdong Kong, Teng Tu, and Weijian Ma.

## Context

NUS is a leading institution for AI research in Southeast Asia, with significant contributions to agentic world modeling. Xuan Billy Zhang serves as project lead for the survey.
