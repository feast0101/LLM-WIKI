# Barbara Oakley

## What

Barbara Oakley is an education researcher and author who created the popular Coursera course "Learning How to Learn." She synthesizes neuroscience research into practical, evidence-based techniques for effective learning and overcoming procrastination. Her work bridges the gap between cognitive neuroscience and practical study strategies.

## Context

Oakley's "Learning How to Learn" provides a comprehensive framework for understanding how the brain learns, combining insights from neuroscience with actionable techniques that anyone can apply. Her approach demystifies learning by explaining the neural mechanisms behind memory formation, consolidation, and retrieval.

## Claims by Source

- Oakley identifies two complementary brain modes for learning: focused mode (concentration) and diffuse mode (relaxed, big-picture thinking). [[sources/learning-how-to-learn]]
- She advocates for chunking, active recall, and spaced repetition as core learning techniques based on how memory consolidation works. [[sources/learning-how-to-learn]]
- Sleep and exercise are critical lifestyle factors that improve learning effectiveness. [[sources/learning-how-to-learn]]
- The Pomodoro technique and focus on process over product are effective strategies for beating procrastination. [[sources/learning-how-to-learn]]
