# University of Oxford

## What

The University of Oxford is a world-renowned research university and institutional home of Kevin Qinghong Lin and Philip Torr, senior authors on the "Agentic World Modeling" survey.

## Context

Oxford contributes significant research leadership to the agentic world modeling survey, particularly through senior author Philip Torr's expertise in computer vision and AI.
