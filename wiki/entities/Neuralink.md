# Neuralink

## What

Neuralink is a company founded by Elon Musk with the mission to develop brain-machine interfaces (BMIs) that enable direct communication between the human brain and computers. The company aims to create technology that would fundamentally extend human cognitive capacity and enable humans to merge with artificial intelligence.

## Context

Within the Wait But Why article, Neuralink is presented as humanity's next major technological frontier—a direct interface to augment and extend human thought itself. The company represents the culmination of human communication technology evolution: from language to writing to printing to the internet, the final step is direct brain-to-computer linkage.
