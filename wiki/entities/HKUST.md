# HKUST

## What

The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology is a major research university and the institutional home of multiple core contributors to the "Agentic World Modeling: Foundations, Capabilities, Laws, and Beyond" survey. Key contributors include Meng Chu, Haoxuan Che, Long Chen, Qifeng Chen, and Wenhu Zhang.

## Context

HKUST is a leading institution for AI and computer science research, with significant contributions to world modeling research as evidenced by multiple core and senior authors on the agentic world modeling survey.
