# Nanyang Technological University

## What

Nanyang Technological University is a major research institution in Singapore and institutional home of multiple contributors to the "Agentic World Modeling" survey, including Ziqi Huang and Wenya Wang.

## Context

NTU contributes research expertise to the agentic world modeling survey, bridging Singapore's contributions alongside NUS and international collaborators.
