# Carnegie Mellon University

## What

Carnegie Mellon University is a leading research university in Pittsburgh and institutional home of multiple contributors to the "Critiques of World Models" paper, including Eric Xing and Mingkai Deng.

## Context

CMU is a prominent center for AI research and machine learning, contributing significant expertise to world modeling and AGI research through its faculty and students.
