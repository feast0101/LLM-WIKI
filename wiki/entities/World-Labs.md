# World Labs

## What

World Labs is a company founded by Fei-Fei Li focused on developing foundational world model technology. The company is at the forefront of spatial intelligence research, building AI systems capable of understanding, simulating, and reasoning about the physical world in three dimensions.

## Context

World Labs represents the commercial instantiation of spatial intelligence research. The company bridges theoretical advances in world models with practical product development. Their flagship product, Marble, demonstrates the company's approach to unifying rendering, simulation, and planning capabilities in a single foundation model.
