# UC San Diego

## What

UC San Diego is a leading research university in California and institutional home of Zhiting Hu, a co-author on the "Critiques of World Models" paper and specialist in machine learning and data science.

## Context

UCSD contributes expertise in machine learning and AI research to the world modeling and AGI research community through its faculty and research institutes.
